# Word Art (ﾉ≧∀≦)ﾉ★

**make your words pop.**

Type a word or a short title, pick Retro 3D, Neon, Chrome or three more styles, and place it on the board as a crisp transparent image. Great for titles, invites and shout-outs, and it stays editable when the words change ✧

![Typing a word into Word Art, clicking through the Retro 3D, Rainbow wave and Neon styles, bending the Neon title with Arc and placing it on the board.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mickyngub/drawdy-word-art/main/docs/media/word-art.gif)

---

## let's shout ٩(◕‿◕)۶

1. Click the **big yellow A** in the extension rail.
2. Type your words in **Text**, like *Hello!*, *Team retro* or *Happy birthday*, up to three lines.
3. Pick a **Style**: each tile previews your words in that style. Click **Place on board** and it lands in view, beside anything already there.
4. To change it later, select it, click **Edit selected**, make your changes and click **Update**. **Place new** makes a copy instead.

> (・_・;) **where did the colours go?** Each style only shows the colours it paints with: Chrome and Rainbow wave have none, and Rainbow wave has no **Arc** either.

---

## make it yours (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧

- **Style**: Retro 3D, Neon, Chrome, Outline, Bubble or Rainbow wave.
- **Font**: Bungee, Pacifico, Righteous, Anton or Rubik Mono One.
- **Colours**: named for what they paint, like **Face colour** and **Depth colour** for Retro 3D or **Glow colour** for Neon.
- **Arc**: bend the words up like a rainbow or down like a smile.
- **Size**: 48 to 200 px.

---

**the small print**: the fonts download from jsDelivr as you use them; nothing you draw or type leaves your board. Your style, font, colours, arc and size stay in this browser; the words live only in the images you place.
